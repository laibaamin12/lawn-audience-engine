Audience analytics for a women's lawn campaign

Lawn Audience Engine

Reads thousands of English, Roman Urdu and Urdu comments from 11.11, 12.12, Eid and winter launch posts, groups them into customer needs, flags posts that behave unusually, and turns the findings into next steps for the marketing team.

Simulated data. Every post and comment here is generated by 01_generate_data.py to demonstrate the method. Brands are labelled A, B and C. No figure describes a real brand. Replace the two CSV files with collected records and rerun the pipeline to get real findings.

Question 3

Which marketing patterns are unusual?

Each post is compared with posts on the same platform and of the same content type. An Isolation Forest flags the 5% that differ most across likes, comments, shares, engagement rate and the mix of complaints. These are leads to investigate, not verdicts.
