Limits to keep in mind

  • The keyword list was written by hand, so slang that is not in it is missed.
  • Roman Urdu spelling varies a lot (kitne, kitnay), which makes matching harder.
  • Sarcasm and mixed-language comments can be read wrongly.
  • Likes and comments show attention, not sales. No sales data is linked.
  • Simulated comments are cleaner than real ones, so real scores will be lower.
  • The page has no simple-rule baseline to compare the anomaly detector against.

Ethics and privacy

  • Collect only public comments, and only in ways each platform permits.
  • Remove usernames, links and phone numbers before analysis.
  • Do not try to identify or contact individual commenters.
  • Report results about groups of comments, not about people.

Glossary

Engagement rate

Likes, comments and shares added together, divided by views. It lets a small post and a big post be compared fairly.

Median

The middle value when posts are sorted. One viral post cannot pull it up the way it pulls up an average.

TF-IDF

A score that gives more weight to words that are common in one comment but rare across all comments, such as "delivery".

k-means clustering

A method that sorts comments into a chosen number of groups so that comments in the same group look alike. Here it makes the audience needs.

Isolation Forest

A method that finds posts which are easy to separate from the rest. Posts that stand out from similar posts get a high score and are flagged.

Robust z-score and the sigma sign

How many "normal steps" a post is from similar posts, measured in a way that ignores extreme values. "+8.0" means about eight steps above normal.

Precision and recall

Precision is how many flagged posts were truly unusual. Recall is how many truly unusual posts were caught.
